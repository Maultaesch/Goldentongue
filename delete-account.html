Delete your account

You can delete your GoldenTongue account and all your data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the app and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

On the website

  1. Log in and open Profile, or open the membership page if you have no membership.
  2. Choose Delete account and type DELETE to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, scores, practice results, saved lines, voice analysis results, conversation practice history and plans are deleted permanently and immediately. Voice recordings are not stored: they are deleted right after the analysis.

Your subscription

Deleting the account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it yourself so you are not charged again: in the App Store (Settings, your name, Subscriptions), in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions), or for website payments in your Stripe receipt email.

Cannot log in?

Contact support from the email address of your account and ask for deletion.
